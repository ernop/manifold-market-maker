# Twitter feature: can adjust image order on the website on PC, by mid 2026

## Finding

The market requires that all users on the normal X website on a PC can reorder attached images without removing and re-uploading them by 2026-06-30. The only drag-to-reorder control reported on desktop web was a two-line drag handle on attached images that belongs to X Premium's long-form posting feature; guides that tested it in January 2026 say free accounts do not get it and that the Android app and mobile web cannot do it either. On 2026-03-30 a Premium user reported that dragging images no longer worked on the website in any of the browsers they tried. X's new multi-image carousel was announced on 2026-07-11, after the close, starting on iOS with web "soon," and the report says nothing about reordering in the web composer. I found no X announcement of image reordering on the website for all users before the close.

## Evidence

- [roboin.io: How to arrange images vertically on X](https://roboin.io/article/en/2026/01/11/how-to-stack-images-vertically-on-x-and-common-issues-explained/) (2026-01-11): images are reordered by dragging a two-line handle at the top left of each attached image; this is "part of the long-form posting feature of X's paid subscription service, X Premium," so free accounts cannot; the Android app and mobile web could not do it in the author's testing.
- [Filtrix: How to Stack Images Vertically on X](https://www.filtrix.ai/blog/how-to-stack-images-vertically-on-x-twitter-complete-guide) (2026-02-04, testing as of January 2026): "Vertical layout is an X Premium exclusive feature... free accounts cannot use this editor"; desktop web is the only place it works.
- [OneImage: The X 'Tap to See It' Vertical Image Trick](https://oneimage.co/blogs/x-vertical-image-trend-tap-to-see-it/) (accessed 2026-09-29): "the drag-to-reorder feature when composing posts. That's a Premium feature"; without Premium you do not get control over the order.
- [r/Twitter: "So X removed it's feature to drag and reorder images inside texts?"](https://www.reddit.com/r/Twitter/comments/1s7kbcz/so_x_removed_its_feature_to_drag_and_reorder/) (2026-03-30): a Premium user reports that dragging images no longer works on the website after trying several browsers, and that earlier posts reverted to a 2x2 grid. This is a single user report, not an official statement.

- X Help Center pages (help.x.com) returned 403 Forbidden to my requests, so I could not check X's official documentation.

## Other side

A YES bettor could point out that the X website did have a drag handle for reordering images during the market window, and that the Postbase guide (November 2025) describes dragging thumbnails on desktop with no mention of Premium. This does not change the recommendation: three guides that describe testing say the handle required Premium, which fails the "for all users" requirement, and a March 2026 user report says dragging then stopped working even for Premium. The Postbase guide gives no testing details and conflicts with those sources.

## Before resolving

On your PC, open x.com, start a new post, attach two images, and try to drag one thumbnail past the other (do not post). If nothing moves, resolve NO. If it moves, note whether your account has Premium: a Premium-only handle still does not meet "for all users," and a control that appeared after the July 2026 carousel rollout would be after the close.
