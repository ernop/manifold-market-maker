# Tesla has agreement with BYD to share access to the supercharger network by mid 2026

## Finding

No Tesla-BYD charging agreement was announced in any region before the 2026-06-30 close. BYD cars can charge at some Superchargers, but only through Tesla's general programs that open sites to every compatible brand: Europe (since 2021), China (a pilot from April 2023 that listed the BYD Han EV and Tang EV among 37 eligible models, expanded to 350+ stations by November 2023, plus WeChat prepaid passes in May 2026), Australia, and Malaysia (March 2026, at the Malaysian government's request). These are one-sided Tesla policies, not an agreement with BYD, and the market requires Tesla and BYD to announce that an agreement is in place. BYD's 2026 charging moves went the other way: it launched its own Flash Charging network on 2026-03-05 and signed a flash-charging deal with Sinopec on 2026-06-03. US tariffs and rules keep BYD passenger cars out of the US and its Canadian launch has slipped toward 2027, so there was no Ford- or GM-style NACS deal either.

## Other side

A loose reading of "share access ... for the majority of the network" could count the fact that BYD owners can use most Superchargers in Europe and many in China and Australia, since about 62% of stalls worldwide were open to other brands by late 2024. This does not change the call: there is no agreement between the two companies and no joint announcement, which is what the title and description require. Open access also predates the market: Europe opened in 2021 and the April 2023 China pilot already included BYD models, so a January 2024 question about an agreement with BYD must mean something beyond the status quo.
