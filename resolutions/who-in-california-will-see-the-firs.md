# Who in California will see the first new plate starting with A on a Tesla

## Finding

The California DMV confirmed in September 2026 that the 9-series (1AAA000 to 9ZZZ999) ended with production of 9ZZZ999, and that the replacement is a "reverse series configuration", 000AAA0, running from 000AAA1 to 999ZZZ9. Plates in the new format have been issued since about March 2026, and the DMV expects the format to last about 40 years, so every new standard passenger plate starts with a digit, not "A". The description says "If ca goes with another solution then we'll NA," which applies here. Ernie already resolved the related market "After California runs out of plates and reaches 9ZZZ999, they'll continue with A" as NO on 2026-08-29, which is the same finding. No one has posted a plate photo in the comments (the latest comment is from 2026-05-14), so there is no candidate winner under any reading.
