# I'll see a big 3 car charging at a Tesla supercharger by mid 2026

## Finding

This resolves on whether Ernie saw a Ford, GM, or Stellantis car charging at a Supercharger by 2026-06-30, which only he can answer. Conditions made a sighting likely: Ford EVs could use Superchargers from 2024-02-29, GM EVs from 2024-09-18, and Stellantis EVs from 2026-03-19, mostly through adapters, and the 2026 Cadillac Optiq and the 2027 Chevy Bolt (deliveries from early 2026) came with native NACS ports. San Mateo has seven Supercharger sites with about 124 stalls, most of which third-party trackers list as open to other EVs, and GM was the second-largest US EV seller in 2025 with about 170,000 sold, while Ford sold about 84,000. PlugShare check-ins show a Ford F-150 Lightning trying to charge at the 2817 S El Camino Real site in San Mateo on 2026-03-18, though it failed because the cable was too short. Over roughly two years of frequent visits, the odds favor at least one sighting, so the lean is YES if he recalls one.

## Other side

NO is plausible. Most Big 3 owners charge at home, Ford drivers do only about 20% of their public fast charging at Tesla sites, GM owners had to buy an adapter and start sessions in an app, and short V3 cables stop some Big 3 trucks and SUVs from reaching (as with the failed Lightning). Stellantis access began only about three months before the close. The market's premise is Ernie's own noticing, so if he did not see one, NO is correct regardless of base rates; the 68% close with 3 bettors adds little.
