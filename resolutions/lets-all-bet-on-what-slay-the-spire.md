## Evidence

- [Slay the Spire 2 - Steam Charts](https://steamcharts.com/app/2868840) (accessed 2026-09-29): monthly average and peak concurrents: June 2026 81,992 / 151,838; July 2026 67,718 / 124,547; August 2026 63,728 / 105,197.
- [Slay the Spire - Steam Charts](https://steamcharts.com/app/646570) (accessed 2026-09-29): June 2026 8,821 / 15,708; July 2026 7,622 / 13,360; August 2026 6,856 / 11,197.
- [Slay the Spire 2 Monthly Charts, SteamPulse](https://steampulse.org/game/2868840/monthly-charts) (accessed 2026-09-29): StS2 daily peaks stayed between about 111,000 and 152,000 on every day of June 2026, which is far above StS1's best month since April 2026.
- [The Architect, Slay the Spire Wiki](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:The_Architect) (accessed 2026-09-29): the Architect is the last Ancient at the end of Act 3. Neow tasks the player with killing him, but that "is not currently possible within the game"; the player deals damage equal to their score and is then killed. The latest change listed is a v0.110.0 beta patch from July 30, 2026, which only removed a multiplayer waiting screen.
- [What will Slay the Spire 2's final ending be like?, PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/card-games/slay-the-spire-2-ending/) (2026-04-02): Casey Yano says the ending is "not too designed at this time" and will come after the alternate acts, characters, and game modes, "probably as we go into V1"; Mega Crit estimates Early Access at one to two years.
- [The new Slay the Spire 2 roadmap has no dates, PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/slay-the-spire-2/roadmap-neowsletter-april-2026) (2026-04-18): the April 2026 roadmap lists "'True Victory' and everything that comes with it" under "further off in the future".
- [The Neowsletter - August 2026, Mega Crit](https://www.megacrit.com/news/2026-8-14-neowsletter-issue-25/) (2026-08-14): Mega Crit is actively working on experimental game modes, an alternate Act 2, and a new character, and there was no August 27 patch.
- [The Neowsletter - September 2026, Mega Crit](https://www.megacrit.com/news/2026-9-25-neowsletter-issue-26/) (2026-09-25): there is no timeline for the next patch, and the newsletter teases a new area of the Spire; nothing about an ending or final boss.
- [Slay the Spire 2: 10 Secrets and Easter Eggs, DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/slay-the-spire-2-secrets-and-easter-eggs/) (2026-09-27): the Architect still "sends us right back to the start", and the Heart from the first game is visible in the background attached to tubes.
- [Timeline, Slay the Spire Wiki](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Timeline) (accessed 2026-09-29): in the in-game lore, the Architect is a separate Ancient whom Neow poisoned with the Blight. The Heart is the creature the StS1 heroes slew, and the lore ties it to the Blight. The demon from the Ironclad's lore orders "KILL THE ARCHITECT!!"
