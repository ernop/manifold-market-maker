# Will a correct solution to the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem be made public before 2027?

Resolves YES if a complete proof resolving at least one of alternatives (A), (B), (C), or (D) of the [official Clay problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/navierstokes.pdf) is publicly accessible before **January 1, 2027, 00:00 UTC**, and that proof is subsequently validated under the rule below. A proof of existence and smoothness or a valid counterexample counts. Human, AI-assisted, and AI-generated proofs all qualify.

**The deadline applies to publication of the correct proof, not to its validation.** A complete correct proof posted on September 10, 2026 and validated in February 2027 counts YES. A press release, private communication, or claim without the complete proof does not establish a qualifying publication date.

Use the earliest publicly timestamped version containing a complete, correct argument: for example, an arXiv version, journal publication, or publicly archived manuscript. Typographical and expository corrections after the deadline are allowed. A new argument, missing case, or substantive repair required after the deadline does not qualify the earlier version. If the earlier version's completeness is disputed, wait for the validation source to address it.

**Validation rule proposed for discussion:** the Clay Mathematics Institute publicly recognizes the specific proof as resolving the problem. Award payment is not required. The market remains unresolved while this validation is pending; no automatic NO merely because the calendar has passed 2026. This deliberately accepts potentially years of waiting and does not substitute the creator's judgment of the mathematics for the named source.

Resolves NO once Clay recognizes a solution and the recognized proof's earliest complete, correct public version is dated on or after January 1, 2027, provided no earlier qualifying proof is still under consideration by Clay. If no solution is ever recognized, the market may remain unresolved indefinitely. There is no automatic NO date in this proposal.

Partial progress, numerical evidence, Euler-equation results, solutions only for modified equations, and results with assumptions insufficient for any alternative of the official problem do not count. If a claimed proof is withdrawn or invalidated before recognition, it does not qualify. Pending disputes delay resolution. Once resolved on the specified evidence, later retractions do not reopen the market.

Betting closes January 1, 2027, 00:00 UTC. Resolution can occur later. Evidence links and the qualifying manuscript version will be stated when resolving.
